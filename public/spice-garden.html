Our Story

A Family Tradition,
Served with Pride.

Spice Garden was born in 2018 when Chef Vikram Malhotra brought his grandmother's recipes from Lucknow to Mumbai. What started as a small tiffin service has grown into one of the city's most-loved dining destinations.

Every spice we use is sourced directly from farms in Rajasthan, Kerala, and Assam. We believe great food starts with great ingredients — and even greater care.

Today, our team of 12 chefs serves over 500 guests each month, but the warmth and soul of home cooking remains unchanged.
